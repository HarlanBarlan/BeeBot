My personal documentation of the process

It is similar to training a dog but the dog doesn't know what it has to do yet:
Its like I am sitting in the kitchen, watching my dog eat my dinner and any time he stops I give him a little treat. He has no idea why I gave him that treat but after enough treats he eventually figures out what he is doing wrong. He doesn't know how to stop it though until I give him some more treats.
